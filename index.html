EPS 2.0 LICENSE AGREEMENT

Section 1 — Parties, Acceptance, Scope and License Grant

1.1 Parties. This Elevation Play Software 2.0 License Agreement (“Agreement”) is between Elevation Software LLC (“Elevation” or “Licensor”) and Tizle Software LLC (“Tizle” or “Licensee”).

1.2 Authorized Acceptance and Effective Date. Breante Glover, acting as Tizle's authorized representative, accepts this Agreement for Tizle through its Elevation-provisioned client dashboard account. The Agreement becomes effective when the representative affirmatively accepts the presented version during initial account setup (“Effective Date”). Elevation records the accepting account identity, agreement version, and acceptance date and time. Setup cannot proceed until that affirmative acceptance occurs.

1.3 Limited License. Subject to Tizle's compliance with this Agreement and payment of applicable charges, Elevation grants Tizle a limited, nonexclusive, nontransferable, nonsublicensable, revocable license during the Agreement term to use the EPS 2.0 Android application, hosted client dashboard, and associated services solely to operate and manage Tizle's own authorized fleet. Elevation provisions the client organization account and a company-specific installation code for authorized devices. Device-count bands determine monthly license pricing and do not themselves restrict the number of activations.

1.4 Permitted Functionality and Access Boundaries. Tizle may use its organization-scoped dashboard to control its own fleet and sessions, manage its own playlist catalog, view its own usage, and receive updates Elevation makes available during the license term. Elevation administers the underlying backend, database, infrastructure, platform and owner controls. This Agreement grants Tizle no source-code access, direct database or backend access, owner-administrator access, resale rights, sublicensing rights, or ownership in EPS.

1.5 Ownership and Client Materials. Elevation retains all rights in EPS software, architecture, source code, platform systems, and associated intellectual property, subject to rights of any third-party licensors. Tizle retains ownership of its playlist entries, catalog information and business records. Tizle authorizes Elevation to host, process and display Tizle-entered information as reasonably necessary to deliver and administer the licensed service. The Agreement's later data provisions will address relevant service records and retention; this section does not promise a data-export function.

1.6 Organization-Level Agreement. Acceptance occurs at Tizle's client dashboard account setup rather than separately in each Android application. Elevation's owner-operated fleet is outside this client license and requires no Tizle acceptance. Tizle may not transfer this license or grant access to another organization without Elevation's prior written permission.

SECTION 2 — AUTHORIZED USE, CREDENTIALS AND DEVICE ACCESS

2.1 Authorized Account. Elevation provisions Tizle's organization account; public registration is not available. The initial license includes one designated client dashboard account, which may operate only within Tizle's organization. Additional accounts are not included. Tizle may request one with a specific business justification, but Elevation may approve or refuse in its discretion and may condition an approved additional account on a separately disclosed charge and applicable access terms. No additional account is promised by this Agreement.

2.2 Credentials and Security. Tizle's designated account holder establishes and maintains their own password using the account-email setup or recovery process. Tizle is responsible for safeguarding account credentials, keeping its account email accessible, and notifying Elevation promptly of suspected unauthorized access. Tizle must not share credentials with another organization or allow access outside the licensed organization. No contractual promise of MFA is made.

2.3 Device Authorization. Elevation provides an organization-specific installation code to authorize EPS on Tizle devices. The code may be reused for authorized Tizle installations and must be kept confidential. Devices use distinct D-number labels within Tizle's fleet. The reserved ROOT label is not available to Tizle. Device-count bands affect charges rather than imposing a fixed technical activation cap. Retirement, replacement, and reinstallation remain subject to Elevation's applicable authorization procedures.

2.4 Permitted Operations. Through its client dashboard Tizle may manage its own EPS sessions, devices, device enable/disable state, playlist entries and usage information, subject to the features and permissions Elevation makes available. Tizle has no permission to administer, view, control, or interfere with another organization's accounts, fleet, catalog, or usage.

2.5 Updates and Service Status. Elevation controls the release and distribution of EPS updates. Tizle may receive updates under Elevation's delivery process. During a license suspension, device operation is disabled when each affected device next successfully communicates with the service; devices that remain offline may not receive that decision until reconnection. Suspension does not itself transfer ownership of client materials or delete the client organization record. Revocation may end dashboard access as addressed in later termination provisions.

2.6 Use Restrictions. Tizle may use EPS solely for its own authorized business fleet. It may not sublicense, resell, transfer, share installation credentials with another organization, circumvent access restrictions, or operate EPS as a service for a third party without Elevation's prior written permission.

SECTION 3 — FEES, INVOICING, PAYMENT AND SUSPENSION

3.1 License Fee and Device Bands. Tizle shall pay a monthly EPS license fee based on the number of registered, non-retired devices in Tizle's organization fleet at the end of the calendar month being billed, measured in America/New_York. Temporarily disabled devices count; devices formally retired or released before that month-end do not. The monthly license fee is $5 for 1–24 devices; $10 for 25–49; $15 for 50–74; $20 for 75–99; $25 for 100–124; and an additional $5 for each further 25-device band. These bands determine price and do not impose a technical device activation cap. Elevation determines the applicable band when preparing the invoice using the fleet's recorded registration and retirement history.

3.2 Usage Charge. In addition to the license fee, Tizle shall pay an initial usage charge of $12.50 per month, beginning with its first month of service, reflecting its allocated share of Elevation's Supabase Pro infrastructure cost. Elevation may change the usage charge by giving Tizle at least thirty (30) days' advance written notice, with the revised amount applying no earlier than the first full service calendar month beginning after that notice period. No usage overage charge is imposed under this Agreement unless the parties separately agree in writing to one.

3.3 One-Time Activation Fee. Tizle shall pay a one-time $9 organization activation fee on its first invoice. This fee is not repeated for the same organization. Any separately approved additional dashboard account and associated disclosed charge are governed by Section 2.1 and are not a repeat of the activation fee.

3.4 Monthly Invoicing. Elevation invoices once per month in arrears for whole calendar months of service, without proration. Elevation manually issues one combined invoice through Mercury on the 21st of the month following the service month, due on the 28th of that invoice month. The first invoice is issued on the 21st of the month following first use and includes the activation fee, the applicable first-month license fee, and the usage charge. At the initial 1–24-device band and initial usage rate, the first invoice is $26.50 and a later monthly invoice without activation is $17.50, subject to any applicable fee-band or usage-charge changes under this Agreement. EPS itself does not issue invoices.

3.5 Payment and Recognition. Tizle must pay each invoice in full by the end of the 28th calendar day of the invoice month, measured in America/New_York (Eastern Time, including daylight-saving transitions). Payment is recognized for EPS entitlement purposes only when the corresponding Mercury invoice reflects Paid status for the invoiced amount; a Processing status does not constitute recognized payment. Elevation may send payment-related notices through its service and account channels.

3.6 Nonpayment and Suspension. If an invoice remains unpaid 48 elapsed hours after the end of its due date, Elevation may suspend Tizle's EPS service. The suspension cutoff is computed using the governing America/New_York timezone. Suspension takes effect for each device when it next successfully communicates with the service; devices that remain offline may not receive the suspension instruction until reconnection. Suspension prevents new EPS sessions and ends affected sessions on successful check-in, but does not itself delete Tizle's organization record or transfer ownership of Tizle materials. No late fee or interest is imposed under the current Agreement.

3.7 Restoration. Once the relevant Mercury invoice is recognized as Paid, EPS restores the organization's entitlement automatically, with affected devices resuming eligibility at their next successful check-in. A pending or processing payment does not itself restore service. This section does not limit other rights of suspension or termination provided elsewhere in this Agreement.

SECTION 4 — SERVICE AVAILABILITY, SUPPORT AND MAINTENANCE

4.1 Online Service and Client Environment. EPS is an internet-connected service using a hosted dashboard and network communications for device check-ins, commands, updates, usage information and license status. Tizle is responsible for providing and maintaining its own devices, connectivity, VPN and access to any third-party playback applications used with EPS. Elevation does not undertake to operate Tizle's fleet or ensure that Tizle's devices are playing at any given time.

4.2 Availability and Third-Party Dependencies. Elevation provides EPS on an as-available basis without a guaranteed uptime percentage, uninterrupted availability, or specified response or repair time. Service operation may be affected by hosting providers, internet networks, Android and other operating-system changes, and third-party playback applications outside Elevation's control. Elevation may undertake maintenance or modify the service to address technical and platform changes.

4.3 Device Communication and Deferred Actions. Device commands and service-state changes depend on successful communication. A device that is offline or cannot reach the service may not receive a command, update, suspension or restoration until its next successful check-in. An unanswered check-in alone is not a suspension or revocation instruction and does not by itself end an already-running session. These communication behaviors do not guarantee continued operation of third-party playback services.

4.4 Technical Support. Elevation provides support on a best-efforts basis for genuine EPS application or licensing problems, billing and payment issues, and EPS functionality. Elevation does not promise a response deadline, resolution deadline, or continuous support coverage. Tizle remains responsible for its own fleet deployment, operation, device conditions, connectivity and playback activity. Elevation will designate the available support submission channel at onboarding and may provide an in-account support facility when available. No presently unbuilt support feature is represented as already operating.

4.5 Updates and Maintenance. Elevation determines the timing, contents and delivery of EPS updates and may provide updates through its controlled release process. No fixed update schedule is promised. Applying an installed update restarts the EPS application and ends any active session, after which a new start command is required. Tizle is responsible for enabling required Android installation permissions and maintaining device compatibility and connectivity necessary to receive updates.

SECTION 5 — DATA HANDLING, CONFIDENTIALITY AND RECORDS

5.1 Hosted Service Records. In providing EPS, Elevation hosts and processes Tizle's organization and designated account information; billing profile, invoice recognition and entitlement information; device registrations, labels, runtime and command/update states; Tizle-entered hosted playlist catalog entries; and service usage records. Support descriptions and screenshots may be processed if Tizle submits them through an available support channel. Apple playlist links stored solely on Tizle devices are not part of Elevation's hosted catalog. Device verification uses public signing-key material; private device keys remain on devices.

5.2 Ownership and Authorized Processing. Tizle retains ownership of its entered playlist catalog and business records, as in Section 1.5. Elevation retains ownership and control of EPS software, infrastructure and its service-operational records, including device, command, entitlement, usage and billing-state records. Tizle authorizes Elevation and its service providers to host, access, process and transmit information as reasonably needed to operate, maintain, secure, bill and support EPS and administer this Agreement. Tizle has no direct database, backend or owner-administrator access.

5.3 Confidentiality and Access. Each party shall use reasonable care to protect the other party's nonpublic business information received through the licensed relationship and shall not disclose it except as reasonably required to perform this Agreement, to professional advisers subject to confidentiality duties, or as required by law. Elevation may grant its authorized personnel and necessary infrastructure, payment and email service providers access to client data for the purposes described in Section 5.2. This provision does not restrict information that is publicly available without breach, already lawfully known, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source.

5.4 Security Practices. Elevation uses access controls and technical measures appropriate to the hosted EPS service, including organization-scoped authorization, authenticated communications and protected account credentials. Tizle is responsible for safeguarding its own credentials, devices and connectivity, as stated in Sections 2 and 4. No absolute security guarantee is made.

5.5 Records, Retention and Export. Device registration and retirement history, billing and usage information and other service records may be retained as part of Elevation's operational and accounting records. Suspension alone does not delete records. EPS does not provide a client self-service export feature or guarantee a downloadable data export. Post-termination retention, access and deletion are addressed in Section 6 and remain subject to an explicit owner policy decision before finalization.

5.6 Incident Handling. Elevation may investigate suspected unauthorized access or security incidents affecting EPS and take appropriate protective measures. Any legally required notifications will be provided as required by applicable law. No separate contractual notification deadline is promised by this clause.

SECTION 6 — TERM, TERMINATION AND POST-TERMINATION EFFECTS

6.1 Term. This Agreement begins upon the authorized acceptance described in Section 1 and continues on a month-to-month basis until ended under this Section 6. There is no minimum commitment or fixed initial term.

6.2 Client Cancellation. Tizle may cancel by giving Elevation written notice through a support or other written contact channel designated by Elevation. Cancellation becomes effective at the end of the calendar month in which Elevation receives that notice, unless the parties agree in writing to a later date. Charges for the service month in which cancellation becomes effective remain due in full under Section 3, without proration, and will be invoiced on the ordinary following-month schedule. Elevation will carry out termination of account entitlement through its operator-administered process.

6.3 Nonpayment Suspension and Termination. Suspension for nonpayment is governed by Section 3 and is not itself termination. If a suspension remains continuously in effect for thirty (30) elapsed days after the applicable invoice's suspension cutoff, Elevation may terminate this Agreement by written notice. The cutoff is determined under Section 3.6, without regard to when any particular device next checks in. If the invoice is recognized as Paid and the suspension is lifted before termination, the right to terminate on that suspension ground lapses. Termination is not automatic, and a new unpaid invoice is assessed separately under Section 3.

6.4 Material Breach and Security. Either party may terminate for the other party's material breach if the breach is not cured within seven (7) calendar days after written notice describing it. Elevation may suspend access or terminate immediately where reasonably necessary to address serious security abuse, unauthorized access or misuse that threatens EPS, other organizations, or its infrastructure, with notice as soon as reasonably practicable. This does not affect Elevation's ability to take proportionate protective measures before termination.

6.5 Effects of Termination. At termination, Tizle's license and right to use EPS end. Elevation revokes the organization's operating entitlement and disables the designated dashboard account through operator administration. An already-installed device receives and enforces revocation at its next successful service check-in; termination does not remotely uninstall EPS from the device. There is no automatic reinstatement after termination; any future service requires Elevation's approval and fresh onboarding. Accrued charges and any provisions intended by their nature to survive termination, including confidentiality and accrued payment duties, remain in effect.

6.6 Client Content and Retained Records. After termination, Elevation will, upon Tizle's written request, arrange manual deletion of Tizle-owned hosted catalog entries, support submissions (if any), and designated-account profile information, to the extent reasonably within Elevation's control and not required to be retained by applicable law. Elevation may retain its necessary service-operational and accounting records, including billing profiles, invoice and payment records, entitlement and suspension history, device registration and retirement history, and usage records supporting billed amounts. Deletion does not reach information stored only on Tizle's devices or records maintained independently by third-party payment providers. No automatic deletion, client self-service export, or post-termination read-only access is promised. Elevation will process deletion requests within a reasonable period, subject to operational and legal requirements.
